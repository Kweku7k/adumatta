About

Designer turned engineer turned founder.

I'm the founder of Presto Solutions, where we automate the everyday operations of African organizations. Our systems work offline-first and over USSD, because that is how most people here connect.

I studied visual arts, then Information Technology at Central University, and came into software through interface design. I started Presto in 2021 while working as an engineer at Rancard and Central University, and went full-time in March 2026.

Online I'm known as the Prampram Elon Musk. I share the building process with 32,000+ followers on TikTok.
